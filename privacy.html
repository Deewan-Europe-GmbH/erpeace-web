Privacy

This website is run by Deewan Europe GmbH (see Impressum). We keep it simple: this website has no tracking, no analytics and no contact forms.

What happens when you visit

Our hosting provider (Netlify, Inc., USA) briefly records technical data such as your IP address and the page you opened, so the website can be delivered and kept safe. We do not use this data to identify you.

Cookies and content from other companies

Today this website shows no content from other companies and sets no cookies. When you choose in the cookie banner, your browser only remembers that choice on your own device, so the banner doesn't come back on every page. That is technically necessary (§ 25(2) TDDDG).

In future, some pages may show videos, maps or posts from other companies (for example YouTube or Google Maps). This content only loads after you allow it with "Allow all" in the cookie banner or with "Load content". Only then does the provider receive your IP address and may set cookies, also in the USA. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time under "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. If we add such content, we will name each provider here.

Our social media profiles

Deewan Europe GmbH and its partner companies run profiles and channels on the platforms below. When you visit them or contact us there (for example by commenting, liking, following or sending us a message), we see your public profile name and what you share with us. We use this to talk with you and present our services. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in communicating with customers and interested people (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If your message concerns a contract or a trial of ERPeace, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR applies.

The platform operators also process your data for their own purposes, such as advertising and statistics, set cookies for this and partly transfer data to the USA. We have no influence on this. See the providers' own privacy policies for details.

  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland): Meta gives us statistics about the use of our pages and profiles ("Insights"). For this we are joint controllers with Meta (Art. 26 GDPR). In an agreement, Meta has taken primary responsibility, including for handling your rights: https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms/page_controller_addendum. We only receive summary statistics, not data about individual people.
  • LinkedIn (LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company, Wilton Place, Dublin 2, Ireland): for the page statistics of our company pages we are joint controllers with LinkedIn (Art. 26 GDPR). The agreement is here: https://legal.linkedin.com/pages-joint-controller-addendum. We only receive summary statistics.
  • YouTube (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland): we publish videos on our YouTube channels and receive summary viewing statistics.
  • WhatsApp Business (WhatsApp Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland): when you message us on WhatsApp, we see your phone number, profile name and messages. Message content is end-to-end encrypted, but WhatsApp processes metadata (for example who writes to whom and when) and shares data with other Meta companies. Please do not send us ID documents or other sensitive data on WhatsApp. You can always reach us by email instead.
  • Podcast on RedCircle and Spotify (RedCircle, Inc., USA; Spotify AB, Regeringsgatan 19, 111 53 Stockholm, Sweden): our podcast episodes are hosted by RedCircle and distributed through Spotify and other apps. When you listen, RedCircle and the app you use process your IP address and technical data, including for listening statistics. We only receive summary statistics.

You can exercise your rights (see "Your rights" below) with us and with each provider. For questions about the statistics it is best to contact the provider directly, because only the provider has access to the user data. We pass on any requests we receive.

When you email us

If you write to us, we use your email only to answer you and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to admin@deewan.eu. You can also complain to a data protection authority.

The ERPeace app itself has its own privacy notice, shown when you sign up.
